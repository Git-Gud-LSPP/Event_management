## 1. Positioning

**Category:** Modular event operations platform.

**What EventHQ actually is (from the codebase today):** the back-of-house system for running events. Events, run-of-show schedules (Gantt), task assignment between organizers and staff, staff rosters, vendors and procurement, inventory, floor plans, incident logging and a document hub. An AI assistant (Ctrl+K) can act across all of it.

**Competitive frame.** Ticketing-first suites (Cvent, Bizzabo, Eventbrite) sell the front of house: registration, tickets, the attendee app. Teams then stitch together spreadsheets, Slack, Drive, a floor-plan tool and a vendor tracker to actually *run* the event. EventHQ starts in that stitched-together operations layer, which nobody owns, and grows outward one module at a time.

**Positioning statement.**
For event agencies and in-house event teams who run several events at once, EventHQ is the event operations platform you assemble from modules. Unlike monolithic event suites that charge for everything up front, EventHQ starts free with the core you need every day, and you add only the modules each event calls for.

**Brand idea: "Build the stack your event needs."** Modularity is the main promise, not a pricing footnote. The visual metaphor is a **patch bay / rigging plot**: modules are nodes, and thin 1px lines connect them into one system. It borrows from technical stage drawings and lighting plots, which event people already read every day.

### FAQ
1. **What's included for free?** Events, tasks, schedule, staff and documents, with 2 active events and 5 seats. No time limit and no card.
2. **How does picking modules work?** Starter lets you turn on any 5 add-on modules and Growth any 20. Swap one module for another whenever you like. Your data in the old module is kept.
3. **What happens when I hit my module limit?** You can swap a module out, or upgrade. We never switch anything off without asking.
4. **Can I downgrade or cancel?** Yes, from Plan & billing at any time. You keep paid access until the end of the billing period, and you can export everything.
5. **Do you charge per seat?** No. Plans include a seat allowance per workspace. Enterprise seats are custom.
6. **Can I move from spreadsheets or another tool?** Import events, staff and vendors from CSV. On Growth and Enterprise, we'll help migrate.
7. **Is the AI assistant included?** Yes, on every plan, with usage limits on Free.
